directly with one specific ECT sector (e.g. emergent gravity,
analogue gravity, MOND-class phenomenology, foundations of QM,
Hubble-tension diagnostics, ultra-diffuse galaxies, BMV/GIE).

**Length target.** 200–280 words. One page maximum.

**Tone.** Direct, technical, non-promotional. Do not pitch the
preprint as a whole; pitch one concrete result and one concrete
question.
