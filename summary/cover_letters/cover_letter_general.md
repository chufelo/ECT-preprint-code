I am sharing an extended summary of a programme in foundational
physics: **Euclidean Condensate Theory (ECT)**.

ECT proposes that Lorentzian spacetime, coherent quantum dynamics,
the gravitational sector, and important parts of the cosmological
and galactic dark-sector phenomenology arise on the broken branch
of a single real scalar condensate $\Phi$ on a four-dimensional
Euclidean manifold, through the spontaneous symmetry breaking
$O(4)\!\to\!O(3)$. Time, the causal cone, and the
quantum--classical split are features selected by the broken phase,
not independent postulates.

Three things may make this useful to you:

1. A **mechanism-first** structure: every headline result is
   presented with its underlying mechanism, what is *not* assumed,
   its rigour level (Level A / B / C / Open), and an explicit
   locator into the full derivation.
2. An explicit **comparison table** with the closest nearby
   frameworks — general relativity / ΛCDM, standard QM, MOND/AQUAL,
   Verlinde-style emergent gravity, Volovik-style analogue gravity,
   CDT, unimodular gravity, and the Standard-Model gauge sector.
3. A deliberately complete **Open list**: Born rule, observed value
   of the cosmological constant, the environment law $\Xi$ for the
   galactic interface, the full electric-charge spectrum, the
   strong-CP problem, and the predictive content of the colour
   completion. ECT is not a finished theory.
