\section*{OP3.3 thermal-completion ledger and KZ scaffold, v2
(proposal-only)}

\subsection*{1. Definitions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\rho_{\rm onset}$: the ordered-branch \emph{effective}
    energy density at branch onset, defined after
    emergence/matching within the FLRW reduction; this is not an
    energy statement at the pre-emergent Euclidean level, where no
    Lorentzian energy bookkeeping applies.
  \item $T_{\rm th}$: the thermal-completion temperature ---
    the temperature at which a standard radiation-dominated bath
    is first established.  No inflationary reheating mechanism is
    assumed; ``reheating'' language is avoided throughout.
  \item $g_*(T)$, $g_{*s}(T)$: standard energy/entropy effective
    degrees of freedom of the bath.
  \item $w_{\rm pre}$: the time-averaged effective equation of
    state of the dominant (condensate) sector between onset and
    thermal completion, within the ordered-branch EFT; the
    existing no-phantom bound gives $w_{\rm pre}\ge-1$, and the
    standard kinetic ceiling gives $w_{\rm pre}\le1$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{2. Core ledger (Level~B conditional bookkeeping
ledger; all items conditional on the FLRW reduction)}

\paragraph{L1 --- Instantaneous-conversion ceiling.}
If at any moment a fraction of $\rho_{\rm onset}$ were converted
into an instantly thermalised bath, its temperature obeys
\[
T_{\max}\le
\Bigl(\tfrac{30\,\rho_{\rm onset}}{\pi^2 g_*(T_{\max})}\Bigr)^{1/4}.
\]
This is a ceiling, not a prediction: if conversion is inefficient
or delayed, $T_{\rm th}\ll T_{\max}$.
Conditional on the Planck-threshold matching estimate for
$\rho_{\rm onset}$, the ceiling is of order $M_{\rm Pl}$.

\paragraph{L2 --- Radiation-domination floor.}
Any thermal completion must deliver
\[
T_{\rm th}\gtrsim T^{\min}_{\rm BBN}\sim\text{few~MeV},
\]
the lowest thermal-completion temperature compatible with standard
BBN and neutrino decoupling; the numerical value is
literature-dependent and must be cited after source verification.

\paragraph{L3 --- Pre-thermal expansion corridor (derived).}
Write $\rho_{\rm th}=\tfrac{\pi^2}{30}g_*(T_{\rm th})T_{\rm th}^4$.
For an approximately constant $w_{\rm pre}>-1$, the dilution law
$\rho\propto a^{-3(1+w_{\rm pre})}$ integrates to
\[
N_{\rm pre}
:=\ln\frac{a_{\rm th}}{a_{\rm onset}}
=\frac{1}{3(1+w_{\rm pre})}
 \ln\frac{\rho_{\rm onset}}{\rho_{\rm th}},
\qquad w_{\rm pre}>-1 .
\]
The case $w_{\rm pre}\to-1$ degenerates (quasi--de-Sitter-like
behaviour) and requires separate treatment; it is excluded from
this relation rather than silently included in the denominator.
[Proposal scratch only; EXCLUDED from article insertion per
review: orientation numbers $N_{\rm pre}\approx65/50/33$ under the
matching estimate.]
For fixed $\rho_{\rm onset}$, $T_{\rm th}$, and
$g_*(T_{\rm th})$, this equation gives only a bookkeeping corridor
for the duration of the pre-thermal ordered-branch epoch.
It is not a prediction of $w_{\rm pre}$ or of $N_{\rm pre}$.

\paragraph{L4 --- $G_{\rm eff}$-settling condition.}
BBN compatibility requires
\[
\bigl|\Delta G_{\rm eff}/G_N\bigr|_{T\sim{\rm MeV}}
\le\varepsilon_G^{\rm BBN},
\]
where $\varepsilon_G^{\rm BBN}$ is an observational tolerance to
be cited after source verification.
[$\beta_\phi$-translation: proposal only; EXCLUDED from first
insertion per review.]
Translating this inequality into a constraint on $\phi_b$ or other
ECT scalar variables is model-dependent and must use the article's
current scalar-tensor closure.
This restates minimal-viable condition~(ii) quantitatively; no
relaxation mechanism is claimed.

\paragraph{L5 --- Entropy compatibility.}
The comoving entropy of the bath at thermal completion,
$S=\tfrac{2\pi^2}{45}g_{*s}(T_{\rm th})\,T_{\rm th}^3\,
a_{\rm th}^3\,V_c$, is, if subsequent evolution is approximately adiabatic,
comparable with the observed entropy in the present observable
Universe, $S_{\rm obs}\sim10^{88}$, after specifying the
comoving-volume convention $V_c$; non-adiabatic late evolution
would modify this accounting.
Combined with L1--L3 this is an accounting identity linking
$(\rho_{\rm onset},w_{\rm pre},N_{\rm pre},T_{\rm th})$; the
mechanism of entropy production is not derived and is not implied.

\subsection*{4. KZ scaffold (Open; definition recorded /
programme sharpened)}
\textbf{Definition (late-stage ordering window).}
Validity domain: an effective Lorentzian ordered-branch
description exists; coarse-grained ordering variables and an
ordering time parameter are meaningful; the passage through the
ordering threshold is characterised by an effective quench-like
rate \emph{in the already Lorentzian ordered branch}.
Input data: relaxation time $\tau(\epsilon)$ and correlation
length $\xi(\epsilon)$ of the ordering variables near threshold;
a freeze-out scale $\hat\xi$, if standard Kibble--Zurek matching
conditions can be justified in the late-stage ordered-branch
regime.
Output targets: a characteristic domain/texture correlation-volume
scale.
For pointlike relic counts one would estimate
$n\sim\hat\xi^{-3}$; for the $\pi_3(S^3)$ texture sectors this is
a domain/texture correlation-volume estimate, not an automatically
conserved defect number.
These estimates \emph{constrain} possible residual gradient/defect
energy contributions that must remain compatible with the ledger
above; they are not an entropy source and do not feed the entropy
budget directly.
\textbf{Open problem (OP3.3, sharpened).}
Exhibit the ECT-native $\tau(\epsilon),\xi(\epsilon)$ from the
ordered-branch EFT and verify the matching assumptions.
No exponents are computed here and no claim is made.
\textbf{Firewall.}
The scaffold applies only after an effective Lorentzian
description exists; it is not an account of the pre-temporal
Euclidean ordering event, which belongs to OP3.1.
