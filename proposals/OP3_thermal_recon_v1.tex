\section*{OP3.3 thermal-completion reconnaissance (proposal-only)}

\subsection*{0. Why OP3.3 and not OP3.1 first}
OP3.1 (the nonequilibrium ordering mechanism; microscopic
$U(\phi),\omega(\phi)$ from bare~P3) is the deepest layer; no
tractable kernel with AN3/Q11-style safety is visible there yet
beyond the existing route remarks.
OP3.2 is already reduced to a conditional criterion (T-reversal
lemma~A, no-bounce sublemma~A-cond., persistence criterion~B);
its residual items are assumptions of that criterion and are
deferred.
OP3.3 has exactly the structure where this session's pattern
works: (a)~a \emph{necessary-conditions ledger} (constraint
statements, not a mechanism --- the AN3/$N_c$ analogue), and
(b)~a \emph{programme formalisation} of the already-gestured
Kibble--Zurek late-stage description (the zero-mode-D1 analogue).

\subsection*{1. Existing anchors in the text}
OP3.3 definition (entropy production, thermalisation, radiation
domination, BBN-compatible initial data) with the explicit caveat
that a KZ-like description may apply only to the late stage and
``cannot be naively extrapolated to the pre-temporal Euclidean
ordering event''; the geometric-onset vs thermal-completion
distinction (Level~A); $\tau_{\rm PT}\sim\ell_{\rm Pl}/c$ under the
Planck-threshold matching estimate; the no-phantom bound
$w_\phi\ge-1$; minimal-viable-branch conditions (ii)--(iv)
(near-frozen $\phi_b$, $G_{\rm eff}\approx G_N$ at BBN, hot
radiation epoch, delayed condensate dominance); ordered-domain
growth analogy (B/C); $\pi_3(S^3)=\mathbb Z$ texture sectors
(app.~relic\_homotopy) as the only primary topological class ---
the natural OP3.4 relic candidates; particle-production section
(OP-Q12, OP3) for rates.

\subsection*{2. Kernel A: conditional thermal-completion ledger
(candidate Level~B constraint package; own formulas)}
Goal: a short list of \emph{model-independent necessary
conditions} that any thermal-completion mechanism must satisfy,
derived from energy bookkeeping, standard FLRW thermodynamics, and
the existing ECT bounds --- explicitly \emph{not} a mechanism.
Draft items (formula sketches; to be derived cleanly in the
proposal v2):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Instantaneous-conversion ceiling.}
    With onset energy density $\rho_{\rm onset}$ (Planck-threshold
    matching estimate), any thermalisation obeys
    $T_{\max}\le\bigl(30\,\rho_{\rm onset}/\pi^2 g_*\bigr)^{1/4}$;
    conditional on the matching estimate, $T_{\max}\lesssim
    M_{\rm Pl}$ --- a ceiling, not a prediction.
  \item \textbf{BBN floor.}
    Completion must deliver a radiation bath with
    $T_{\rm reh}\gtrsim$ few~MeV (literature value to be
    verified at source: Hannestad-type bound $\sim4$--$5$~MeV);
    combined with item~1 this defines the admissible corridor.
  \item \textbf{Pre-thermal equation-of-state corridor.}
    Between onset and thermalisation the background is dominated
    by the condensate sector with $w_{\rm pre}\in[-1,1]$, the
    lower end enforced by the existing no-phantom bound; the
    expansion (e-folds) of the pre-thermal epoch then maps onto
    $(\rho_{\rm onset},T_{\rm reh},w_{\rm pre})$ by standard
    dilution algebra $\rho\propto a^{-3(1+w)}$ --- an explicit
    relation to derive, giving a duration/e-fold bound for each
    $w_{\rm pre}$.
  \item \textbf{Stiff-epoch side condition.}
    If $w_{\rm pre}>1/3$ (kination-like condensate kinetic
    domination), the relative blue-tilting of any primordial
    gravitational-wave background and $N_{\rm eff}$-type bounds
    constrain the duration --- include only as a flagged
    conditional item with literature verification, or defer.
  \item \textbf{$G_{\rm eff}$-settling condition.}
    BBN compatibility requires $\phi_b$ (hence $G_{\rm eff}$)
    near-frozen by $T\sim$~MeV: an upper bound on the condensate
    relaxation timescale relative to the pre-BBN Hubble time ---
    restates minimal-viable condition~(ii) as a quantitative
    inequality.
  \item \textbf{Entropy budget.}
    The produced comoving entropy must account for
    $S_{\rm obs}\sim10^{88}$; with items 1--3 this fixes the
    minimal converted energy fraction as a function of
    $(w_{\rm pre}, N_{\rm pre})$ --- bookkeeping identity, no
    mechanism.
\end{enumerate}
Honest framing: every item is conditional on the FLRW reduction
and (where used) the Planck-threshold matching estimate; the
package \emph{constrains} OP3.3, it does not close it.

\subsection*{3. Kernel B: KZ late-stage scaffold (Definition /
Open-problem block; all Open)}
Formalise the existing one-sentence KZ remark into:
\textbf{Definition (late-stage ordering window).}
Validity domain: ordered Lorentzian description available;
coarse-grained ordering variables and an ordering time parameter
meaningful; quench characterised by an effective rate through the
ordering threshold.
Input data: relaxation time $\tau(\epsilon)$ and correlation
length $\xi(\epsilon)$ of the ordering variables near threshold;
freeze-out scale $\hat\xi$ from the standard KZ matching.
Output targets: residual gradient-energy fraction and defect
density $n_{\rm def}\sim\hat\xi^{-3}$ feeding (i)~the entropy
budget of Kernel~A and (ii)~the OP3.4 relic programme, with the
$\pi_3$ texture sectors as the only primary topological class.
\textbf{Open problem (OP3.3, sharpened).}
Exhibit the ECT-native $\tau(\epsilon),\xi(\epsilon)$ from the
ordered-branch EFT and verify the matching assumptions; no
exponents are computed here and no claim is made.
\textbf{Firewall (verbatim from the existing text, strengthened):}
the scaffold applies only after an effective Lorentzian
description exists; it is not an account of the pre-temporal
Euclidean ordering event, which belongs to OP3.1.

\subsection*{4. What is NOT proposed}
No transition mechanism; no derivation of $T_{\rm reh}$, of the
radiation content, or of CMB acoustic structure; no KZ exponents;
no extrapolation of KZ to the pre-temporal event; no change to
OP3.1/OP3.2 statuses; no claim that the ledger closes OP3.3.

\subsection*{5. Risk register}
Forbidden: ``ECT derives reheating/thermal completion'';
``the transition produces the observed CMB'';
``KZ applies to the Euclidean ordering event'';
``$T_{\rm reh}$ predicted''.
Safe: ``necessary-conditions ledger, conditional on the FLRW
reduction''; ``ceiling/floor/corridor, not a prediction'';
``scaffold definition, exponents open''.
Main hazard: importing inflationary-reheating intuition; the
ledger must use only conservation laws, dilution algebra, and the
cited observational floors.
