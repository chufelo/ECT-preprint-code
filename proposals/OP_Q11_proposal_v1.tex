\paragraph{Proposition Q11.1a: OS spin--statistics for a free
Euclidean $Spin(4)$ spinor kernel (candidate Level~A; mathematical
statement).}
For a local quadratic Euclidean Dirac kernel transforming in a
half-integer representation of $Spin(4)\simeq SU(2)_L\times SU(2)_R$
and satisfying the standard Osterwalder--Schrader reflection
structure, antisymmetric (Grassmann) quantisation yields a
reflection-positive reconstruction.
Conversely, symmetric quantisation of the same local half-integer
spinor kernel violates reflection positivity.

\emph{Proof sketch.}
(\emph{Direct.})
For the Grassmann Gaussian sector with kernel
$S_E=(-\gamma^E_A\partial_A+m)\,C$, the OS form
$\langle(\Theta F)^{*}F\rangle_E$ for $F$ supported in the
half-space $w>0$ reduces, via the half-space transfer-matrix
construction, to a form
$\langle\xi,\,e^{-(w_1+w_2)\hat h}\,\xi\rangle$ with
$\hat h\ge 0$, which is non-negative; this is the standard
Osterwalder--Schrader fermionic construction adapted to the ECT
normalisation $e^{-S_E/S_0}$ and the reflection $\Theta w=-w$
of \S\ref{sec:qs_reflection_positivity}.
(\emph{Converse.})
If the same kernel is quantised symmetrically, the two-point
Schwinger function must be permutation-symmetric and is therefore
given by the symmetrised kernel.
Locality and $Spin(4)$ covariance force the first-order
($\gamma^E\!\cdot\!\partial$) structure of any local half-integer
quadratic kernel; under the OS reflection the symmetrised kernel
produces a quadratic form that is not of definite sign, and an
explicit smearing function $f$ supported at $w>0$ with
$\langle(\Theta\psi(f))^{*}\psi(f)\rangle<0$ can be exhibited in
momentum space.
[Full momentum-space computation to be supplied in the
implementation round; until then the converse direction carries
``candidate'' status.]\hfill$\square$

This is a mathematical statement about the free Euclidean spinor
kernel.
Its ECT application is conditional until the ECT spinor quadratic
kernel and its reflection-positive structure are derived or
explicitly verified from the condensate inputs
(Q11.1b, Level~B conditional); the Dirac form of
\S\ref{sec:dirac_eq} currently enters with Level~B inputs, and the
reflection-positivity results of
\S\ref{sec:qs_reflection_positivity} cover the free quadratic
scalar subsectors only.
The composite/effective character of the ECT fermionic fields does
not invalidate the route, but it precludes claiming a
first-principles Level~A ECT derivation of fermionic statistics at
this stage.

\paragraph{Q11.2: descent to the ordered branch (Level~B
conditional).}
The $Spin(4)\to Spin(3)$ breaking pattern carries the half-integer
representation content into the ordered Lorentzian branch.
This group-theoretic descent is not by itself a spin--statistics
theorem.
The descent additionally requires:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item compatibility of the OS reflection $\Theta$ with the
    ordered-branch time $t=w$;
  \item continuity of the half-integer representation content
    across the breaking pattern $Spin(4)\to Spin(3)$;
  \item ordinary $3{+}1$-dimensional exchange topology,
    $\pi_1(\mathcal M_2)=\mathbb Z_2$, with no effective anyonic
    sector for fundamental exchange in the primary branch;
  \item no topological-defect sector changing the exchange class;
  \item inherited locality/domain-of-dependence and
    positive-Hilbert-space reconstruction in the ordered branch;
  \item the proof is performed in the symmetric Euclidean/OS sector
    and transported into the ordered branch; it is not derived from
    the broken $O(3)$ structure alone.
\end{enumerate}
