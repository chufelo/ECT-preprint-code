\section*{0. Scope and firewall}

$\phi_b$/$u$-dependencies in the current preprint (759\,pp state of 2026-06-11) and
construction of the dimensionless locking matrix with a five-way status
classification (derived / assumed / unknown / protected / dangerous). All
observational bounds quoted below are order-of-magnitude stress anchors pending a
dedicated citation pass. The four-layer separation (local screened values;
cosmological background values; laboratory observables; dimensionless ratios) is
enforced throughout. No preprint text is touched; Step~3 (protection/screening
analysis) and any D1 blocks are downstream and review-gated.

\section{The decisive structural observation}
\textbf{(a) Screening cannot rescue the cosmological channels.} Quasar
$\Delta\alpha/\alpha$ and H$_2$ $\Delta\mu/\mu$ measure the \emph{cosmological
background} at $z\sim1$--$3$ --- precisely where the retained $\varepsilon$-band
asserts $u(z)\neq u_0$. The screened-local/cosmological split therefore cannot
protect $\alpha$ or $\mu$ in these channels even in principle. Outcome~B of the
recon (screening extension) is available only for \emph{local} observables (clocks,
LLR, Oklo); for the cosmological channels only Outcome~A (structural zeros) or
Outcome~C (a quantified bound) exist.

\textbf{(b) P-anchored vs B-tracking classification.} The audit reduces Question Q1
to a sharp dichotomy. Call a matching coefficient \emph{P-anchored} if it is fixed
by bare action parameters (e.g.\ $\phi_0=\sqrt{\mu^2/\lambda}$, the postulate-level
$\alpha,\beta,c_*$), and \emph{B-tracking} (with exponent $p$) if it follows the
local/cosmological background amplitude, $\propto u(X)^p$, as the recorded induced
stiffness chain $\kappa_n=\mathcal C_n u_0^2$ does ($p=2$). Then:
$\zeta_A\in\{0\ (\text{P-anchored}),\ \sim2\ (\text{B-tracking, induced-kinetic
analogy})\}$ and $c_{v_2}=-\partial\mathcal I_{\rm EW}/\partial\ln u$ with the
$\phi_0$ factor P-anchored by construction. \emph{Step~3 is therefore reduced to two
derivative computations}: the anchoring class of $Z_A$ (phase-sector kinetic
normalisation) and of $\mathcal I_{\rm EW}$. The $p\simeq2$ case is a deliberately
conservative stress scenario borrowed from an induced-stiffness pattern, not a
derived or preferred model of $Z_A$.

\textbf{Conclusion of the stress layer.} Within the retained $\varepsilon$-band,
\emph{any} $O(1)$ background-tracking of $Z_A$ or of the $v_2$-chain would be in
severe tension with the quoted stress anchors (factors of order $10^{5}$) in
cosmological channels that screening cannot reach. The theory
therefore \emph{requires} structural (P-anchored) protection of $\alpha$ and $\mu$
--- or a correspondingly suppressed $\varepsilon$-sector. This is the sharpest
consistency pressure identified in the programme to date, and it is decided by the
two Step-3 computations above.

\section{Findings and flags}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt,label=\textbf{F\arabic*.}]
\item $c_G=-1$ is \emph{derived} (eq:Geff\_phi); the $Gm_p^2/\hbar c$ row of the
matrix \emph{is} the $\varepsilon$-sector, already constrained by the recorded
five-probe machinery. Internally consistent.
\item $c_\alpha$ is \emph{unknown} and decisive: the $\zeta_A$ dichotomy
(P-anchored vs B-tracking) is screening-proof in the quasar channel. The recorded
induced-stiffness analogy ($\kappa_n=\mathcal C_n u_0^2$) supplies a deliberately conservative stress scenario with
$p\approx2$; it is not a derived or preferred model of $Z_A$, and the question must
be settled by derivation, not by assumption.
\item $c_{v_2}$ is \emph{unknown} with the same decisiveness via $\mu$; the danger
localises entirely in $\partial\mathcal I_{\rm EW}/\partial\ln u$, since the
$\phi_0$ factor is P-anchored by construction.
\item The screening law itself is recorded as qualitative (L$\sim$7721); even
Outcome~B for the \emph{local} channels (clocks, LLR, Oklo) requires the
quantitative screening derivation before it can be invoked.
\item A charge-\emph{ratio} protection theorem is essentially already available
(integer lattice + $\mathbb Z_6$ interlock): a cheap formal statement is a
candidate Step-3 deliverable. It protects ratios, not the magnitude of $\alpha$.
\item Gap flag: the varying-constants channels (quasar $\alpha$, H$_2$ $\mu$,
Oklo, clocks) are absent from the retained five-probe set (Hubble$+r_s$, JWST, CC,
$f\sigma_8$, ISW); OP-CONST closes a real omission. Related bookkeeping question:
the uniform-$\varepsilon$ ansatz is declared late-time while the Probe-1 module
integrates to $z_*\approx1090$; the validity window should be stated explicitly at
Step~3 (no claim made here).
\item Bookkeeping ambiguity: identifying the closure-level $G_{\rm eff}\propto
u^{-1}$ with the matching-level $G=c_*^4/(8\pi M_G^2)$ mixes two layers; the
$u$-scaling of $M_G$ (and hence of $m_e/M_{\rm Pl}$) requires a unified closure-map
statement before that row of the matrix can be completed.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Outcome assessment and Step 3 definition}
No D1 block is prepared at this stage; Variant A/B/C of the recon remains
\emph{undecided} and is now reduced to a minimal, well-posed task list:
(i)~derive the anchoring class and exponent of $Z_A$ from the phase-sector kinetic
normalisation (Outcome A requires $\zeta_A=0$ structurally);
(ii)~derive the $u$-dependence of $\mathcal I_{\rm EW}$ (Outcome A requires
$\partial\mathcal I_{\rm EW}/\partial\ln u=0$);
(iii)~formalise the charge-ratio protection statement (F5);
(iv)~derive the quantitative screening profile needed for the local channels (F4);
(v)~state the validity window of the uniform-$\varepsilon$ ansatz (F6).
Only after (i)--(ii) can a D1 block be drafted, under the recon firewall and
external review. Items (i)--(ii) are pure derivations on recorded structures and
are the natural first front of the next working cycle.
