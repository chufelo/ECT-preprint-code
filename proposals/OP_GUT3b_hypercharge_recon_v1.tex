\section*{OP-GUT3b reconnaissance v1: what can normalise
hypercharge? (proposal-only)}

\subsection*{0. Scope honesty}
OP-GUT3b is recorded as ``derive observed charge assignments of
quarks and leptons'' and is Open.
Nothing here derives those assignments.
The deliverables are (i)~a map of the recorded $Y$-fixing
structure, (ii)~a four-notion separation of what ``hypercharge
normalisation'' can mean, with each notion assigned to its
recorded status and owner problem, and (iii)~two candidate
computable items for v2.

\subsection*{1. What the article already records}
(a)~\emph{Anomaly layer (from the OP-GUT2 rounds):} the local
anomaly system fixes the one-generation hypercharges only up to
the recorded solution family; with a free-hypercharge $\nu^c$ a
$B{-}L$ flat direction opens, so ``the anomaly system alone no
longer fixes hypercharge''; an SM-like family exists for every odd
$N_c$.
(b)~\emph{Lattice layer (OP-GUT3a, partially closed):}
Theorem~thm:charge\_lattice (Level~A): the emergent Abelian sector
is compact by construction, so charges sit on a lattice
$q=n\,e_0$; the electroweak $\mathbb Z_2$ refinement
$2T+Y\in2\mathbb Z$ is recorded at Level~B.
(c)~\emph{Three-level distinction already recorded} in the
charge-quantisation section: conservation (done) /
lattice quantisation (done, Level~A) / full observed pattern
$Q=T_3+Y/2$ with specific values (Open: needs electroweak
embedding, hypercharge quantisation, representation content).
(d)~\emph{Coupling-magnitude layer:} the gauge-kinetic
normalisations are expected from condensate stiffnesses
($Z_B^{(0)}\!\leftarrow\!K_\theta^{\rm eff}$,
$Z_W^{(0)}\!\leftarrow\!\kappa_n$), with the Weinberg angle a
separate open problem (OP-GUT7) and couplings-to-condensate
OP-GUT4.

\subsection*{2. Four distinct notions of ``hypercharge
normalisation'' (the proposed sharpening)}
\emph{(N1) Rational pattern:} the ratios
$Y_Q:Y_{u^c}:Y_{d^c}:Y_L:Y_{e^c}$ within a generation.
Recorded status: fixed by anomalies only up to the solution family
and the $B{-}L$ flat direction.
Owner: this is the core of OP-GUT3b.
\emph{(N2) Lattice unit:} on which discrete lattice $Y$ lives,
and how that lattice interlocks with the electric lattice
$q=n\,e_0$ and the non-Abelian centres.
Recorded status: Abelian lattice Level~A; electroweak
$\mathbb Z_2$ interlock Level~B; the colour interlock is
recorded only as an \emph{open pointer} --- the
charge-quantisation section's ``What remains open'' list already
notes (with citation) that the full pattern likely depends on the
global structure $(SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)_Y)/\Gamma$,
$\Gamma=\mathbb Z_6,\mathbb Z_3,\mathbb Z_2$, and that
$\pm e/3$ charges require the colour sector --- but no computed
interlock requirement is developed.
Owner: OP-GUT3a extension, feeding OP-GUT3b.
(Correction note: an earlier draft of this recon wrongly stated
the pointer was absent; the session grep itself located it.)
\emph{(N3) Coupling magnitude:} the normalisation of the
hypercharge kinetic term ($g'$, equivalently $Z_B^{(0)}$, the
GUT-style $k_Y$ factor).
Recorded status: structural stiffness expectation; quantitative
closure under OP-GUT4/OP-GUT7.
Owner: explicitly \emph{not} OP-GUT3b; conflating N3 with N1/N2 is
the main category error this recon guards against.
\emph{(N4) Convention:} $Q=T_3+Y/2$ versus $Q=T_3+Y$ etc.;
bookkeeping only, one sentence at most.
Consequence: ``what fixes the normalisation of $Y$ if not
anomalies'' splits cleanly --- N2 is fixed by compactness/interlock
structure (largely recorded or recordable), N3 by stiffness ratios
(different problem), and the genuinely open OP-GUT3b core is N1
pattern selection \emph{within} the N2 lattice.

\subsection*{3. Two candidate computable items for v2}
\emph{(L1) Lattice-interlock requirement (centre-quotient
structure).}
Combining the recorded Level-A electric lattice $q=n\,e_0$ with
the observed fractional quark charges $\pm\tfrac23 e,\mp\tfrac13e$
forces $e_0=e/3$ \emph{and} a correlation between the
charge-lattice class and the colour representation (only colour
triplets occupy the $e/3$-classes) --- the standard
centre-quotient ($\mathbb Z_6$-type) global structure.
This would \emph{develop the article's own recorded open
pointer} (the $/\Gamma$ item of the ``What remains open'' list)
into a computed consistency requirement on the OP-GUT1 colour
completion, with the recorded electroweak $\mathbb Z_2$
refinement ($2T+Y\in2\mathbb Z$) as its already-recorded
$\mathbb Z_2$ part.
Status if computed: a consistency requirement on completions, not
a derivation of $Y$.
\emph{(L2) Lattice versus the $B{-}L$ flat direction.}
The recorded flat direction shifts $Y\to Y+c\,(B{-}L)$ with
continuous $c$; imposing the N2 lattice discretises $c$ but does
not eliminate the flat direction.
Computable question for v2: state exactly what survives
(discrete lattice automorphisms within the anomaly family) and
record that residual freedom honestly --- this would sharpen
OP-GUT3b's statement from ``derive the assignments'' to ``select
within an explicitly characterised discrete family''.

\subsection*{4. What this recon does NOT do}
No derivation of the observed assignments; no $\sin^2\theta_W$
claims; no claim that compactness fixes the Standard-Model
charges; no claim that OP-GUT3b is solvable inside the current
class; no insertion.

\subsection*{5. Risk register}
Forbidden: ``ECT derives the hypercharges''; ``compactness fixes
the SM charge pattern''; treating the coupling magnitude (N3) as
part of OP-GUT3b; presenting L1 as new physics rather than the
standard global-structure statement imported as a requirement.
Safe: ``four notions separated; owners assigned'';
``consistency requirement on completions'';
``selection within an explicitly characterised discrete family''.
