\subsection*{3. Findings}
\paragraph{F1 (mechanical; three sites): O1--O3 vs O1--O5
extent.} The subsection and the registry define the reformulated
OP-$\Lambda$3 programme as O1--O5 (five registry rows; closing
summary ``O1--O5 \ldots constitute the reformulated
OP-$\Lambda$3 programme''), but three main-text echoes say
\emph{three}: L26303 (``listed as O1, O2, O3''), L45888--45889
(``splits into three technical open problems (O1--O3)''),
L46200 (``O1--O3 \ldots; alternate label: OP-$\Lambda$3'').
Likely O4/O5 were added later. Proposed minimal fix: at the two
narrative sites, ``the three decoupling-side core tasks
(O1--O3), completed by the realization-side tasks O4--O5'';
at L46200, simply ``O1--O5''.

\paragraph{F2 (wording): Theorem~1(v) ``If and only if''.}
The ``if'' direction is proved; the converse is demonstrated for
the scalar-prefactor class $Z(\phi)$ only. Proposed: ``If'' in
the theorem statement, plus ``conversely, a generic violation
(e.g.\ a scalar prefactor $Z(\phi)$) reintroduces the
coupling''; alternatively keep ``if and only if'' with an
explicit genericity qualifier.

\paragraph{F3 (optional): no forward pointer to O5.} The theorem
corollary and the convention note restrict to the leading
ansatz; a parenthetical ``(beyond-leading corrections: O5)''
would close the loop.

\paragraph{F4 (mechanical; appendix only; two sign sites).}
The 4D identity is $\det(\sqrt{-g}\,g^{\mu\nu})=g$, not $-g$
(verified numerically on a random Lorentzian metric: both sides
$=-0.036448$); hence $\det K=g_{\rm eff}<0$, and
$\sqrt{-g_{\rm eff}}=\sqrt{-\det K}=\sqrt{\beta^3(\alpha-\beta)}
=\beta^2/c_*$. App.~app:rank1 currently states
``$\det(\sqrt{-g}\,g^{\mu\nu})=-g$'' and
``$\det K^{AB}=-g_{\rm eff}$'' yet then evaluates
$\sqrt{\beta^3(\alpha-\beta)}$, i.e.\ it is internally off by one
sign in the prose while the final value is correct. The main
subsection (eq:sqrt\_g\_const) already uses the correct
$\sqrt{-\det K^{AB}}$ form. Fix: two sign corrections in the
appendix; no result changes.
