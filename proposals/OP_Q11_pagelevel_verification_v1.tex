\subsection*{1. Already verified at page level (full texts read)}
\textbf{(V1) Kikukawa--Usui, arXiv:1012.5601 (full text).}
Their eqs.~(16)--(17):
$(\theta\bar\psi)(x)=\gamma_0\psi(\theta x)$,
$(\theta\psi)(x)=\bar\psi(\theta x)\gamma_0$ ---
\emph{verbatim} our eq.~q11\_eucl\_theta\_spinor.
Their eqs.~(18)--(19): $\theta(FG)=\theta(G)\theta(F)$, antilinear
--- verbatim ours.
Their eq.~(21): explicit $\theta$ of a general monomial (order
fully reversed, $\gamma_0$ attached per field) --- consistent.
Their eqs.~(13),(22): weight $e^{-S}$; positivity stated as
$\langle\theta(F_+)F_+\rangle\ge0$ (eq.~23).
Their eqs.~(72)--(73): same reflection for Majorana fields,
compatible with Majorana conditions --- supports our
Majorana-translation caveat.
\textbf{(V2) Kikukawa--Usui, arXiv:1005.3751 (excerpt).}
Action convention $e^{+A(\bar\psi,\psi)}$ --- demonstrates that
action-sign/pairing conventions vary across sources within the same
school; supports our appendix (b) wording.
\textbf{(V3) hep-lat/0306014, Appendix~B (excerpt).}
Site reflection positivity for Wilson fermions stated as
$\langle F_+\,\Theta_s F_+\rangle\ge0$ --- the \emph{opposite}
order to (V1) appears in the literature as well; again consistent
with the global-$\sigma$/pairing equivalence of our appendix (b),
and a caution against claiming a unique literature order.
\textbf{(V4) Williams, CMP 38, 65 (1974), introduction (excerpt;
contemporaneous primary source).}
Characterises the OS-1973 construction explicitly: OS obtained the
Feynman--Kac formula ``at the expense of \emph{doubling the fermion
fields}, and of a \emph{non-Hermitean Euclidean action}.''
Mapping: our independent Grassmann generators $(\psi,\bar\psi)$ are
the Berezin form of exactly this doubling; the non-hermitean action
is the standard $\bar\psi(\gamma_A\partial_A+m)\psi$ we use.
