\section*{OP-GUT1 zero-mode programme, v2 (proposal-only)}

\subsection*{0. Scope}
Sharpens the ``Definition (the colour zero-mode problem) / Open
problem (OP-GUT1, sharpened)'' block of \S colour\_completion into
a workable programme.
Everything below is Open; nothing upgrades P7; OP-GUT1 remains
Open/Reframed.

\subsection*{1. Working requirements for $\mathcal D_{\rm defect}$}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Locality: a differential operator built pointwise from the
    minimal condensate data $(\rho,\theta,n^A)$ and their
    derivatives; no external structure.
  \item Working class --- first pass: elliptic first-order
    Dirac-type operators, chosen because index tools control their
    kernels.  Higher-order or non-Dirac operators are outside this
    first pass, not excluded.
  \item Equivariance under the residual ordered-branch symmetry
    $O(3)\times U(1)_\theta$.
  \item A fixed $U(1)_\theta$ charge assignment on the domain
    bundle: the $\theta$-action is a natural candidate source of
    the complex structure on the kernel, because the colour module
    required by P7 is complex rather than real.
  \item Spectral gap separating the would-be zero modes from the
    continuum on the relevant backgrounds, so that
    $\ker\mathcal D_{\rm defect}$ forms a smooth bundle over
    spacetime and the Berry--Wilczek--Zee connection is defined
    (no level crossing along physical paths).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{2. Admissible backgrounds (grounded in the existing
ontology)}
Per \S ``Types of defect and topological configurations'' and
app.~relic\_homotopy:
(i)~$\theta$-winding / vortex-core configurations.
These $\theta$-winding cores are not primary $O(4)\to O(3)$
strings; they rely on the compact phase fibre and require separate
stability analysis --- there is no conflict with
$\pi_1(S^3)=0$.
(ii)~$\pi_3(S^3)=\mathbb Z$ texture/skyrmion sectors --- the only
nontrivial homotopy class of the primary breaking
($\pi_1=\pi_2=0$: no primary strings, no monopoles).
(iii)~Domain-wall-like / interface configurations, including
ordered--Euclidean interfaces at the BR1 boundary.
(iv)~Composite configurations.
The ontology itself is marked ``not yet fully classified''; the
programme inherits that conditionality explicitly.

\subsection*{3. Route taxonomy (no commitments)}
\textbf{R1 --- index/charge route ($\theta$-winding backgrounds;
schematic).}
In suitable Jackiw--Rossi/Callias-type settings, the index can
count zero modes in terms of winding/charge data, schematically
$\dim\ker\sim|n|$; the applicability of any such counting to an
ECT-native operator depends on mass profile, asymptotics, boundary
conditions, and Fredholmness, none of which are established here.
Honesty statement (retained): R1 would \emph{reduce} rank-3 to the
stability/selection of charge-3 cores --- a charge-selection
question (energetics of binding three elementary cores?) that is
itself entirely open --- it would not solve rank selection.
References (Jackiw--Rossi; Callias; optionally E.~Weinberg) to be
added only after page-level verification.

\textbf{R2 --- residual-symmetry degeneracy diagnostic
(high-risk).}
One may ask whether an internal kernel degeneracy could transform
as a three-dimensional multiplet under a residual symmetry
naturally available in the ordered branch.  The tempting candidate
is the vector representation of residual $O(3)$, later
complexified by a $U(1)_\theta$ charge.  This is not yet a colour
construction: spatial rotations cannot simply be identified with
an internal colour gauge symmetry, and any viable mechanism must
explain why the resulting threefold degeneracy is an internal,
gaugeable fibre rather than an ordinary spatial vector multiplet.
The observations $\Omega\sim\varepsilon_{ijk}$ and
$N_c=3\sim d_{\rm spatial}=3$ may be recorded only as diagnostic
prompts, not as explanatory claims.
This route is useful mainly as a warning: any
$d_{\rm spatial}=3$ argument must pass the internalisation test.

\textbf{R3 --- interface route.}
Localised modes on ordered--Euclidean or inter-branch interfaces
(Jackiw--Rebbi-type localisation); counting data unclear; least
developed; listed for completeness.

\textbf{R4 --- $G_2$ reduction.}
Nested deeper candidate ($u^\perp\simeq\mathbb C^3$); deferred to
the separate $G_2$ mini-round by review decision; listed only for
completeness of the taxonomy.

\subsection*{4. The induced-$(h,\Omega)$ sub-problems, made
precise}
\textbf{(h).}
For elliptic $\mathcal D_{\rm defect}$ the fibrewise $L^2$ inner
product on $\ker$ supplies $h$ automatically; the substantive
content is smoothness of the kernel bundle (requirement~5), not
the metric.
\textbf{($\Omega$): the determinant-line / trivialisation
problem.}
For a smooth complex rank-3 kernel bundle
$E=\ker\mathcal D_{\rm defect}$, an $SU(3)$-structure requires a
Hermitian metric and a nowhere-vanishing complex volume form
$\Omega\in\Gamma(\det E^*)$, equivalently a trivialisation of the
determinant line $\det E$.
The topological obstruction is measured by $c_1(\det E)$;
vanishing $c_1$ is the first necessary global condition, while a
canonical $\Omega$ would require an additional preferred
trivialisation, plausibly tied to condensate orientation data.
Thus the question ``why $SU(3)$ rather than $U(3)$'' becomes a
precise determinant-line/trivialisation problem, not a solved
result.
\textbf{(BWZ).}
The colour connection is the Berry--Wilczek--Zee connection of the
smooth kernel bundle.
A BWZ connection on a kernel bundle gives a candidate gauge
connection; it does not by itself derive the Yang--Mills action,
the coupling $g_s$, or confinement (kinetics partially addressed,
separately, by the minimal colour EFT section).

\subsection*{5. Failure modes (recorded honestly)}
Generic $\dim\ker=0$ on natural backgrounds; kernel real with no
$\theta$-complexification; $|n|=3$ selection unjustified (R1);
failure of the internalisation test (R2): threefold degeneracy
that remains a spatial multiplet is not colour; level crossing
destroying the bundle; nontrivial $c_1(\det E)$ or no preferred
trivialisation; and even full success would leave Yang--Mills
kinetics, $g_s$, confinement, and OP-GUT2 representations open.
Consistency check inherited from the $N_c$ classification: any
successful mechanism must produce a complex triplet, and the
classification guarantees that no anomaly argument can substitute
for it.
