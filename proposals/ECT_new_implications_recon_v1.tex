leading-order vanishing (protection) theorem is derived \emph{before} any nonzero
correction is discussed.

\section{The ECT structural-laws programme (meta-frame)}
The central deliverable of this reconnaissance is not a list of exotic effects but a
systematic table of \emph{universal protection / no-go / selection laws}. For every
sector the four governing questions are:
(i)~which phenomena are \emph{forbidden} by the ordered-medium architecture;
(ii)~which are forbidden only \emph{conditionally} (and on what);
(iii)~what is the \emph{first allowed correction} and its suppression;
(iv)~which \emph{existing} laws of physics are reborn as ECT protection theorems.
Charter members:
no-drag theorem; constants-protection theorem (or its honest failure bound);
no-dangerous-Lorentz-violation beyond the recorded $|\delta c/c|$ anchors;
vanishing of higher-order interference (Sorkin sector) in C1--C3 regimes;
no-leading-order GW birefringence unless specific parity-odd/anisotropic terms are
derived to exist; equivalence-principle universality with the $\mu_5$-pattern as the
unique allowed violation channel.

\section{Priority 1 --- OP-CONST: coupled drift and protection of constants}
\label{sec:opconst}
\textbf{Goal.} Either a protection theorem for the dimensionless constants under
ordered-branch background drift, or an honest, quantified limitation entry --- found
by us before any referee.

\textbf{Step 1: matching-function inventory} (locate and freeze the recorded
dependencies in the preprint): $G_{\rm eff}(\phi_b)$ and the screened-local vs
cosmological split (Scenario A/B, $\dot G_{\rm eff}/G_{\rm eff}=-\beta_\phi\dot\phi$);
the $\varepsilon$-sector map $G_{\rm eff}(z)/G_N=(1+z)^{2\varepsilon}$; the
compact-$U(1)$ normalisation $Z_A(\phi_b)$ entering $\alpha$; the electroweak
condensate $v_2$ entering $m_f=y_f v_2/\sqrt2$ (status: external input per the
EW-scale audit); $S_0$ and $c_*$ (enter only through dimensionless combinations);
the colour scale under P7 background-level triviality (so $\mu=m_p/m_e$ drifts mainly
through $m_e/\Lambda_{\rm QCD}$, i.e.\ through $v_2$ and $Z$-factors).

\textbf{Step 2: the locking matrix in invariants.} Derive
\[
d\ln\mathcal O_i \;=\; c_i\, d\ln\phi_b,\qquad
\mathcal O_i\in\{\alpha,\ \mu,\ G m_p^2/\hbar c,\ m_e/M_{\rm Pl},\dots\},
\]
with each $c_i$ an explicit functional of recorded matching data. The ECT-specific
content is the \emph{vector of ratios} $c_i/c_j$: standard physics has no such
locking.

\textbf{Step 3: constraint set} (test layer only; firewall rule~2):
atomic-clock drifts $|\dot\alpha/\alpha|\lesssim10^{-17}\text{--}10^{-16}$\,yr$^{-1}$
and $|\dot\mu/\mu|$ at the same level; Oklo ($z_{\rm eff}\sim0.14$); quasar absorption
$|\Delta\alpha/\alpha|\lesssim10^{-6}$ at $z\sim1$--$2$; LLR
$|\dot G/G|\lesssim10^{-12}$\,yr$^{-1}$ (already recorded); BBN.

\textbf{Step 4: the crux.} The retained band $\varepsilon\in[0.0296,0.0376]$ implies
$\Delta\ln G_{\rm eff}=2\varepsilon\ln(1+z)\approx4$--$5\%$ to $z=1$, i.e.\ a naive
$\langle\dot G/G\rangle\sim3\times10^{-12}$\,yr$^{-1}$ --- at the LLR edge, and
survivable only through the recorded screened-local/cosmological split. \emph{Question
Q1: does the same screening mechanism extend to $Z_A(\phi_b)$ and $v_2(\phi_b)$?}
Three outcomes: (i) $c_\alpha=c_\mu=0$ structurally (protection theorem; strongest
result); (ii) nonzero $c_i$ but universal screening (must be proved, not assumed);
(iii) conflict $\Rightarrow$ quantified bound on the $\varepsilon$-sector and an
honest limitation entry.

\textbf{Deliverables.} Locking-matrix table; protection-theorem draft or limitation
entry; explicit statement of which outcome obtains; no preprint contact before
review.
