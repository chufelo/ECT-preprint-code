## Candidate scope

This successor package aligns the English ECT publication repository with the
R189-reviewed manuscript chain and introduces a standalone-clone-safe,
offline, two-phase release freeze:

- the technical preprint remains the canonical claim owner;
- the narrative companion and compact summary remain downstream;
- Level A / Level B / Level C / Open status is preserved;
- exact sources, PDFs, BBLs, and build reports are frozen before tagging;
- a separate attestation verifies the exact local Git commit and tag after
  they exist;
- Zenodo payloads are staged from the attested commit, not from an arbitrary
  worktree; and
- mixed licensing and third-party redistribution boundaries are explicit.

## Scientific status

No claim is upgraded by this release preparation. Model-internal derivations
remain conditional on their stated assumptions. Structural closure routes
remain Level B where physical matching owners are missing. Fits, imported
benchmarks, and application diagnostics remain Level C. Missing actions,
states, vertices, measures, likelihoods, and observable maps remain Open.

The package separates deterministic reproduction from experimental agreement
and from ECT-specific discrimination.
